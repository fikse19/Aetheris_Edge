Executive Partner Briefing

Aetheris Edge × AST SpaceMobile

Core Challenge

Non-Terrestrial Network (NTN) direct-to-cell links suffer from variable satellite RTT (100–300ms) and packet jitter, leading to voice call degradation (MOS drop) and high backhaul bandwidth costs.

Aetheris Technical Solution

Software-defined traffic prioritization and inline stream compression at the edge ingress boundary before payload transmission across the space link.

Target PoC Metrics

  • Ingress Latency Overhead: < 4.2 ms
  • Payload Compression: ~35% Bandwidth Reduction
  • VoIP Quality: Maintains Jitter Buffer stability with MOS > 4.2
