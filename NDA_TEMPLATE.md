# Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement

This Mutual Non-Disclosure Agreement ("Agreement") is entered into by and between Top Cover Group and the Client for the purpose of evaluating the Aetheris Edge technology platform.

## 1. Purpose
The parties agree to protect confidential information disclosed in connection with the Pilot Evaluation Program.

## 2. Confidential Information
Confidential Information includes all non-public technical, business, financial, product, security, and operational information, whether oral, written, visual, or electronic.

## 3. Obligations
Each party shall:
- use Confidential Information solely for evaluation purposes;
- restrict disclosure to employees and advisors with a need to know;
- use at least the same degree of care as it uses for its own similarly sensitive information.

## 4. Exclusions
Confidential Information does not include information that is publicly known, independently developed, lawfully received from a third party, or required to be disclosed by law.

## 5. Term
This Agreement remains in effect for a period of three (3) years from the date of last disclosure, unless terminated earlier by written notice.

## 6. Governing Law
This Agreement is governed by the laws of the jurisdiction governing the parties’ business relationship, without regard to conflict of law principles.

## 7. Acceptance
By executing or acknowledging this Agreement, each party confirms its commitment to protect the confidentiality of shared technical and operational data.
